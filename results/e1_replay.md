**Correction (2026-10-02).** The first version of this table had two bugs in
`analysis/replay.py`:

* **Private selection** made its final choice among group winners using the
  *true* means, which is oracle information. It reported a ranking accuracy of
  1.000 / 0.972.
* **SVT** compared every candidate with the fixed baseline M₀ instead of the
  current incumbent. It returned the last accepted candidate rather than the
  incumbent, and had no threshold noise. It reported 0.001 / 0.003.

Both mechanisms now run as sequential loops on released information only.
`tests/test_replay.py` checks that they are near chance at a negligible budget
and near-perfect at a huge one. The earlier mechanism ranking is withdrawn.

These are synthetic replay outputs used to prioritise implementation. They are
not LLM results and do not establish a DP-RAE advantage. Private selection
uses the conservative Liu–Talwar calibration (3ε₀ per event); a fixed-size
report-noisy-max would need less noise.
